# Exam Preparation

DeutschGarden includes two original mini mocks for every level from A1 to C2. Each mock combines objective reading/listening items with guided writing and speaking tasks.

## Module timing reference

The UI provides current Goethe-Zertifikat-style preparation timings:

| Level | Reading | Listening | Writing | Speaking |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A1 | 25 min | 20 min | 20 min | 15 min |
| A2 | 30 min | 30 min | 30 min | 15 min |
| B1 | 65 min | ~40 min | 60 min | ~15 min |
| B2 | 65 min | 40 min | 75 min | 15 min |
| C1 | 65 min | ~40 min | 75 min | ~20 min |
| C2 | 80 min | ~35 min | 80 min | ~15 min |

Always verify provider requirements before a real exam because formats can change.

## Content policy

- Mini mocks are independently authored for DeutschGarden.
- Official examination papers are not copied into the repository.
- Exam-provider names are used only to describe the preparation reference.
- Objective mini-mock score is persisted.
- Writing and speaking include task checklists; offline self-assessment is not presented as an examiner score.
