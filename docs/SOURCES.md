# Reference Sources

DeutschGarden content is independently authored. The following external materials are used only as framework/exam-format references.

## Council of Europe — CEFR

- CEFR overview and Reference Level Descriptions: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/reference-level-descriptions
- CEFR resources: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages

Key design implication: CEFR itself is language-independent; language-specific Reference Level Descriptions can inventory words, forms and grammar by level. This is why DeutschGarden does not claim a universal official word count for each CEFR band.

## BAMF and federal law — LiD / citizenship test

- Official test regulation and format:
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/einbtestv/BJNR164900008.html
- Official question catalogue (Anlage 1):
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/einbtestv/anlage_1.html
- LiD and citizenship-knowledge thresholds:
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/inttestv/__10.html
- BAMF learner information and catalogue PDF:
  https://www.bamf.de/DE/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Einbuergerung/einbuergerung-node.html

Unlike Goethe practice papers, this official catalogue is redistributed. The
questions are published as an annex to a federal regulation and German
copyright law § 5 excludes laws and regulations from copyright protection. The
checked-in extraction is Stand 07.05.2025, includes no third-party translation
or explanation, and carries the MIT image-extraction notice at
`assets/civics/NOTICE.md`. See `docs/CIVICS_TEST.md` for the reproducible import
and cross-check procedure.

## Licensing note

The app does not bundle third-party dictionary dumps discovered during
research. This avoids silently imposing incompatible content licenses or weak
CEFR-level assignments on the user’s project. The official civics catalogue
above and its explicit extraction notice are the documented exception.
