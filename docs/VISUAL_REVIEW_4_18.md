# Advanced noun review 4.18.0

29 new drawings (24 C1, 5 C2) and 25 declined candidates. This is a reviewed tranche; the full C1/C2 sweep remains unfinished.

The drawings were rendered at 40 and 96 pixels in a browser grid and compared with their nearest silhouettes and colours across the existing library. The 24-pixel neighbour ranking is a way to find comparisons, not a semantic quality score. Teleskop, Edelstein, Akrobat and Lacrosse drafts failed review and were removed. The urn was redrawn as a closed ceramic vessel after its first pedestal form looked like a trophy. No generated-scene or line-icon tier is overwritten.

## Example corrections

Pokal: corrected *Unsere Mannschaft hast* to *Unsere Mannschaft hat*. Aktentasche: added the comma before the infinitive group. Teddybär: corrected the dative to *mit seinem Teddybären*. Grip: replaced the unrelated *Grips* example with tyre traction. Raupe: replaced the misleading butterfly-as-adult-caterpillar sentence with a metamorphosis example. Feuerlöscher: supplied its plural, *die Feuerlöscher*.
