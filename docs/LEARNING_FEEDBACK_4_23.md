# Learning feedback in 4.23

## Question practice

Civics and driving practice and mocks now offer read-aloud and random-question
controls. Read-aloud speaks the German stem and displayed choices, without
identifying the answer. It can be stopped; changing question or leaving stops
it. The controls remain alive while scrolling through a long question.

Opening practice or changing topic selects the first unseen item. Random
selection prefers unseen items, then unanswered mock items, then other review
items, while avoiding an immediate repeat. It stays inside the current filter
or fixed mock. Timers, selected answers, question composition and scoring do
not change. The new question controls do not change placement or language-exam navigation.

The additive `seenTestQuestionIds` profile field records rendered questions,
not whole generated tests. It is merged by union in backups and cleared by full
progress reset. Known older answers supply partial exposure history; visits
that were never stored cannot be reconstructed. Viewing alone grants no XP,
mistake or review grade. New/seen badges describe the current visit.

## Optional sound feedback

Five original PCM WAVs are generated by `tool/generate_ui_sounds.py`. They are
short, quiet synthesized tones, not recordings or externally licensed assets.

| Cue | Event | Duration |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Click | Main navigation, question navigation and selection | 30 ms |
| Success | Correct vocabulary, grammar, listening, reading and practice answers | 210 ms |
| Failure | Retry feedback for incorrect answers or unsuccessful graded attempts | 220 ms |
| Achievement | Newly completed daily quests | 300 ms |
| Completion | Passed lessons/mocks, completed assessment or audio-course day | 540 ms |

Interface sounds has its own Settings switch, independent of German speech.
The preference is local in safe backup merges. Silent/unsupported audio cannot
block learning or profile writes. The player is lazy: it opens no native audio
plugin during startup. Android effects request no audio focus; iOS uses the
ambient category. Effects stop when the app leaves the foreground. Both
microphone backends hold a shared quiet gate and drain effects before capture,
with a bounded wait so a broken player cannot block the recorder. Pending
effects cannot start late during recording or after muting/disposal.

Rapid taps are coalesced and lower-priority effects cannot cut off completion
feedback. Correct/incorrect cues take precedence over a quest notification.
The ungraded pronunciation lab does not acquire a sound-based pass/fail grade.

## Visual and language guidance

Two original text-free SVGs illustrate guided practice and completion. They
are paired with ordinary labels, not used as the only explanation of status.
The Learn page and question hubs offer collapsible listen–recall–check advice;
primary practice actions remain ahead of optional guidance. The shared lesson
completion page also uses the illustration. Phone layouts are checked in both
interface languages at 320 pixels and 150% text.

Fifteen authored notes add German examples, an English explanation/translation
when the interface is English, and a prompt to produce an original sentence.
They cover 290 of 460 civic and 39 of 72 driving stems. They are offered after
practice feedback, not as answers to a live mock. Whole-word matching avoids
mistaking compounds such as Kanne/Wohnung/Erwerb for kann/wo/wer. They are short
learner notes, not a full grammar analysis or a new scoring system.

Modal-verb and wo-preposition explanations were checked against the primary
IDS grammis references on [modal verbs](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/380)
and [prepositional adverbs](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/523).
Examples and explanations here are newly authored; no corpus passages are copied.
Official civic question wording, answer keys and legal thresholds are unchanged.

`test_question_tools_test.dart` covers persistence, backup merging, topic/state
boundaries, fixed mocks, answer retention, timers, speech cancellation and
missing speech. `ui_sound_player_test.dart` covers quiet capture, pending-play
cancellation, muting, priorities, unavailable devices and waveform limits.
`question_learning_content_test.dart` covers language matching, post-answer
visibility, sound preferences and narrow translated layouts. The existing
full suite, translation/content gates and actual Android release startup smoke
test remain mandatory release gates. Waveforms are reproducible with:

```sh
python tool/generate_ui_sounds.py --check
```
