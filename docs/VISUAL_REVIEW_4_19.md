# Advanced noun review 4.19.0

42 C1/C2 noun candidates were reviewed. 22 receive original drawings (18 C1, 4 C2); 20 are declined with individual reasons. The full advanced-level sweep remains open.

The SVGs were rendered at 40 and 96 pixels in a browser grid, checked alongside silhouette/colour neighbours across the existing drawing set, and compared with the relevant object or action cues. Pixel rankings are review aids, not semantic proof. Generated scenes take priority and none of these new noun cards is hidden by that tier. Emoji mappings were regenerated after the drawings were finalized.

The bulb was revised to unlit glass with a visible filament so it differs from the glowing idea symbol. The marmot was revised to a profile with a small ear, muzzle and incisors so it does not read as the new teddy bear. The X-ray and chimney drafts were rejected after comparison with Röntgenaufnahme and Schlot.

The same decisions appear in `tool/vocab_icons_undrawable.tsv`. An intentional decline does not mean the card loses its structural cue, word-class label, or a suitable existing emoji.

## Definition corrections

Statur now reads “physique, build, stature (physical appearance)” rather than the unrelated character/integrity senses. Its existing example is about a person's body build. Verified against [Duden: Statur](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Statur).

Lebensjahr now reads “year of one’s life” rather than “at the age of”. The English phrase was a translation of the example construction rather than a noun definition. Verified against [Duden: Lebensjahr](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Lebensjahr).
