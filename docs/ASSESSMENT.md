# Placement Assessment

## Purpose

The placement test estimates a useful learning starting point from A1 through C2. It is **diagnostic**, not a certification examination.

## Structure

Each CEFR band contains six objective items:

- 2 vocabulary
- 2 grammar
- 1 reading
- 1 listening

Total item bank: 36.

## Adaptive routing

1. Begin at A1.
2. Complete the six items in the current band.
3. A band score of at least 67% advances to the next band.
4. If the learner does not reach that criterion, the recommendation is the last successfully demonstrated band (or A1 if A1 itself is not passed).
5. Passing C2 reports C2.
6. The app also reports overall attempted-item accuracy and domain breakdowns.

## Why this is not a certified CEFR result

A six-item band sample cannot reproduce a full standardized language examination. Productive writing and speaking normally require richer tasks and human or validated automated scoring. The result is therefore used only to choose a starting point inside the app.

## Persistence

The most recent placement level, score and date are stored locally. A placement result can unlock levels up to the recommended band.
