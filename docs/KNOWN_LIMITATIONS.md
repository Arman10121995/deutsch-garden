# Known Limitations

**Current speech and visual status:** German platform ASR explicitly requests German even when
the OS cannot enumerate locales; an advertised roster without German now asks
for a language pack. A physical Windows microphone/playback check is complete; population accuracy
and other devices remain unvalidated.
Story quotes use authored roles, radio interludes retain their host and device
voice fallback no longer inherits the previous speaker. A single installed
German voice still separates roles only by pitch. The current semantic SVG
count is 2,391 (23.91% of the deck, plus 52 generated scenes). The library was
audited and weak drawings were redrawn in 4.15; 4.16 added 750 further drawings.

1. The bundled vocabulary bank contains 10,000 training cards. Their A1–C2 labels are curriculum decisions, not an official CEFR word-count table: CEFR provides no universal fixed vocabulary threshold, and the full deck has not been psychometrically calibrated against a certified learner population.
2. **Placement results recorded before 4.2 are not trustworthy.** Every one of the sixty placement questions was authored with the correct answer first, so the test could be passed at 100% by tapping the top option without reading anything. 4.2 permutes the options, and results taken from that release onward are measuring what they claim to. Earlier profiles were left alone rather than reset — silently discarding someone's recorded level would be its own kind of wrong — so a learner who placed implausibly high can retake the test or set their level directly in Profile. Placement uses a 60-item adaptive instrument (ten per band, of which most learners see six) and has not undergone psychometric norming against a large certified learner sample. Since 3.24 a band stops as soon as a Wilson interval at 80% confidence separates the learner from the 67% pass mark, and carries on to the reserve items when six could not. Where ten answers still straddle the mark the result says so rather than presenting the band as measured. Two things this does not fix: multiple-choice items cannot distinguish a confident learner from a lucky one at the margin, and the 80% operating point is a judgement about a low-stakes recommendation, not a calibrated error rate.
3. Listening uses synthesised German rather than studio-recorded audio. Windows, macOS, iOS and Linux run the five bundled German Piper voices through sherpa-onnx. Android 4.5 deliberately avoids sherpa after reproducible native crashes in long generation: it renders installed German OS voices into a private WAV, prefers voices marked offline and falls back to pitch separation if only one exists. The web build similarly selects available installed German browser voices when available. Dialogue, quoted story speech and Gartenradio preserve explicit speaker turns and 850 ms gaps, but none of these paths provides regional accents, spontaneous connected speech or the prosody of actors responding to one another. Dedicated listening lessons, the audio course, stories and radio now use the same full transport player; the anticipation drill remains a separate productive exercise.
4. Writing evaluation is an offline transparent heuristic and cannot replace trained human scoring for advanced CEFR production. Since 4.17 capitalization feedback is conservative: it checks known nouns and clear nominal uses, rather than assuming every word after an article or pronoun is a noun. Keyword and connector matching is still a language heuristic, not semantic understanding or a full grammar parser.
5. **The speaking tutor is a scripted role-play, not a language model.** It follows a fixed sequence of authored turns and evaluates each reply with a deterministic rule engine. It will not improvise, will not answer a question you ask it, and will not follow you off-script. What it does reliably is tell you whether you addressed the turn, at sufficient length, using the target structures.
6. **The pronunciation lab is ungraded.** A physical Windows pilot exposed that the synthetic MFCC/DTW mapping can reject spoken sentences while accepting near-silent microphone noise. Since 4.22 the lab displays timing observations and explicitly labelled recognised-word agreement, without acoustic percentages, stars, pass/fail, graded XP, retention events or review scheduling. Practice time still counts, and existing profile history is preserved. A conservative -80 dBFS RMS input floor rejects digital near-silence before level normalisation or offline recognition; it is not a general speech/noise classifier. Raw acoustic distances remain available to the private validation runner. Representative learners, independent blinded German-speaking raters and held-out acceptance are still needed before introducing a pronunciation grade.
7. **Speech recognition on Linux needs the optional model.** `speech_to_text` has no Linux implementation, so there is no transcript there without the optional German model. Recording and ungraded timing feedback work independently of transcription. Since 4.1, Settings offers the ~105 MB CC-BY-4.0 desktop model. It is the app's only requested network download and is not offered on Android or iOS; see #18. Recording on Linux needs `pulseaudio-utils` and `ffmpeg`.
8. The iOS artifacts CI produces are **unsigned**. The release includes an `.ipa`-shaped archive and the raw `.app`, but neither carries a provisioning profile or trusted signature; putting either on a device needs re-signing with your own Apple identity.
9. Speech recognition quality is the platform's, not the app's, wherever the platform's is what runs. On Android the system recogniser may route audio to the vendor's servers unless a German offline language pack is installed; on some devices and emulators no recogniser exists at all. Every speaking screen therefore accepts typed input as an equal path. On desktop the optional downloaded model is preferred over the platform recogniser when it is installed, for exactly that reason — but its accuracy on learner speech is itself unestablished; see #18.
10. Free-talk scoring checks, since 3.24, whether the vocabulary of each content point actually appears in the answer: every prompt carries authored German keyword sets, matched through a light stemmer that folds umlauts and inflectional endings, and coverage is 60% of the score. An off-topic answer of the right length now scores under 45 and is told which points it missed. What this still cannot do is comprehend: someone who names their sister without saying anything about her counts as having covered “who is in your family”, a bare thesis with no marker is detected only through its topic vocabulary, and a keyword used to deny a point counts the same as one used to make it. It detects the vocabulary of a point, not the point.
11. Stories are original graded texts written for this app, not authentic published literature. They are level-controlled rather than natural.
12. The SM-2 implementation uses conventional parameters rather than parameters fitted to this learner population; it is a sound default, not a tuned model. Since 3.24 every grade is recorded in an append-only review log, which now powers exact misgrade undo and honest retention statistics and makes future fitting possible. What is still missing is enough consented, representative history plus a validated fitting/evaluation process; changing scheduler parameters merely because data now exists would be experimentation on learners, not calibration.
13. Intervals are spread by up to five per cent since 3.24, so a session's cards no longer all come due on the same later day. The spread applies from three days upward; shorter intervals are left alone because moving a one-day interval by a day is a different schedule rather than a spread. The grade buttons deliberately preview the unspread number. What is still true is that the *parameters* underneath -- the SM-2 ease steps -- remain untuned, for the reason in #12.
14. Achievements, quests and streaks are local. There is no leaderboard, social feature, account or cloud sync, so nothing here is comparable across users. Moving a profile between platforms remains a manual export/import. Since 3.24 the default import safely reconciles item progress and de-duplicates review events, so two diverged copies can be merged without discarding one side; it is still a user-initiated transfer rather than automatic synchronisation.
15. Exam-prep mini mocks train task types and strategy but are not full-length official provider papers.
16. Real exam formats can change. Verify official provider pages before registration or final preparation.
17. Card meanings can be shown in Turkish as well as English since 3.24, but only for 478 concrete nouns — 4.8% of the deck — and **those translations have not been reviewed by a native speaker.** They were produced by the same process that wrote the rest of the app. The automated check verifies that every entry points at a real card, that none is blank, and that none is still the German word; it cannot verify that the Turkish is right. Everything else falls back to English, which is the gloss the deck was authored with. The authored semantic SVG count is now 2,391 (23.91% of the deck), with 52 additional generated scene illustrations for concrete actions and states; visual coverage and translation coverage are intentionally separate gates.
18. **Speech recognition accuracy across learner German is not established.** The 2026-10-19 physical Windows check collected ten speaker-verified attempts, including wrong-sentence and silence controls, using the actual recorder, bundled Thorsten reference, offline model and playback. The speaker confirmed hearing both reference and recorded playback. This identified engineering defects and supports one-PC functional acceptance, not population accuracy or cross-platform microphone acceptance. Raw recordings, speaker transcripts and detailed measurements remain private outside Git. The optional NVIDIA German FastConformer model remains a second opinion: inspect its transcript against what you actually said. Neither its word agreement nor the synthetic acoustic mapping grades pronunciation practice. Platform recogniser comparison, independent human ratings, representative speakers and noisy/device coverage remain outstanding. See [SPEECH_VALIDATION.md](SPEECH_VALIDATION.md).
19. **Hints are still heuristic where there is no authored rule.** Grammar lessons use their own rule; vocabulary uses the card's category, gender, masked example, lapse history and learner mnemonic; earlier skips and wrong choices change the first hint. Listening and reading comprehension infer whether to look for time, place, reason, person, quantity, case or word order. That is substantially more personal than the old generic fallback but it is still deterministic pattern matching, not a tutor that understands why a particular learner is confused.
20. **A hint cannot leak the answer, but it can be useless.** `lib/hints.dart` drops any candidate hint containing the correct option and falls back to a weaker one, which is enforced by `tool/check_hints.py` and by tests against the real content. What is not guaranteed is that the surviving hint actually helps: for a question whose rule text had to be dropped, the learner gets a generic pointer instead. Measured on the shipped grammar lessons, over 90% of questions get a hint of some kind, but the split between rule hints and structural fallbacks is not tracked and no learner has been asked whether either helped.
21. **The driving-theory module is a study aid, not an official question bank.** It uses 72 original bilingual Class B questions and an official-format-inspired 30-question mock. German licensing rules and current official materials can change; use the current authority or a driving school for final preparation. Its English helper is machine-generated learner support, not an official translation.
22. **Civics English is also a helper, not a legal translation.** Every bundled LiD/Einbürgerungstest question has a locally generated English rendering, but the official German catalogue remains the source of truth. The app stays offline because the translations are shipped as an asset; no translation model runs on the device.

23. **Interface and authored teaching text have different language rules.** 4.22 completes the remaining C3 guide/title/outcome migration: 28 interface files and 1,771 matching English/German keys, including all 417 lesson titles and 72 course-unit titles/outcomes. German exercise material, examples, stories, glossary notes and explicit English study helpers retain their authored languages. The coverage/placeholder gate and narrow-screen tests do not establish independent editorial fluency.
