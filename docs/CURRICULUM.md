## A1

Focus: identity, family, home, food, time, shopping, transport, basic work/study, simple questions and transactions. Grammar covers present tense, verb-second order, pronouns, articles, accusative basics, frequent dative patterns, modal verbs, separable verbs, imperative, negation, coordination, time–manner–place and introductory Perfekt.

## A2

Focus: routine social communication, appointments, travel, health, services, everyday correspondence and simple narration. Grammar expands Perfekt, dative, two-way prepositions, adjective patterns, subordinate clauses, reflexive verbs, comparisons, Präteritum of high-frequency verbs, relative clauses, zu-infinitives and indirect questions.

## B1

Focus: independent everyday/professional communication, connected narration, opinions, complaints, planning and familiar public topics. Grammar covers full adjective-declension foundations, genitive, relative clauses across cases, passive, Konjunktiv II, temporal clauses, Plusquamperfekt, pronoun order, governed prepositions and connected discourse.

## B2

Focus: extended argument, workplace/academic topics, media, policy, evidence interpretation and formal correspondence. Grammar emphasizes passive/modal passive, advanced adjective/relative structures, Konjunktiv, nominalisation, participial attributes, concessive/conditional linking, N-declension, complex infinitive clauses and information structure.

## C1

Focus: precise professional/academic communication, synthesis, evaluation, inference, stance and register control. Grammar includes reported speech, functional-verb constructions, nominal/verbal style shifts, extended participial attributes, advanced prepositions, correlative connectors, modal particles, negation scope, complex comparison, cohesion and register-sensitive word order.

## C2

Focus: nuanced conceptual argument, subtle inference, rhetorical control, synthesis of competing perspectives, high-register reformulation and ambiguity management. Grammar includes marked information structure, ellipsis, evidential/epistemic modality, counterfactual past, complex infinitive clusters, rhetorical nominalisation, reported-perspective control, pragmatic mitigation, idiomatic prepositional patterns, focusing constructions and style transformation.

## Progression

Normal progression unlocks the next level at 65% overall mastery across the six skills. A diagnostic placement result can unlock a higher starting level, preventing forced repetition for experienced learners.
