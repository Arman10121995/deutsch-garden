# Placement Instrument Blueprint

Construct: practical starting-level estimate for DeutschGarden.

Bands: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2.

Per band:
- vocabulary: 2 selected-response items
- grammar: 2 selected-response items
- reading: 1 context-based comprehension item
- listening: 1 TTS-delivered comprehension item

Advance criterion: 4/6 (66.7%) or better.

Interpretation: last supported band. The app does not claim equivalence to a standardized certificate examination.

Known limitations:
- small item count per band
- no productive skill scoring in placement
- TTS rather than human-recorded listening audio
- no item-response-theory calibration or normative sample

Future validation path:
- pilot with learners holding recent certified CEFR results
- estimate item difficulty/discrimination
- increase item pool and randomize equivalent forms
- evaluate test–retest stability and misclassification rates
