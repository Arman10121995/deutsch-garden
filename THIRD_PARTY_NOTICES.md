## Leben in Deutschland / citizenship-test catalogue

The German questions are official federal material published as Anlage 1 to
the Einbürgerungstestverordnung. The validated text and images are distributed
with the source and extraction details, non-affiliation statement, and the
complete MIT notice for the image extraction in:

- `assets/civics/NOTICE.md`
- `docs/CIVICS_TEST.md`
